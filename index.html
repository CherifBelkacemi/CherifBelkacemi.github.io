About me

I am a PhD student in financial mathematics and actuarial risk in a CIFRE partnership between BPCE Vie, CERMICS at École nationale des ponts et chaussées, and LIRSA at Le Cnam. My academic supervisors are Benjamin Jourdain and Stéphane Loisel.

My research concerns the stochastic behaviour of life-insurance policyholders and efficient Monte Carlo methods for asset–liability models. I am particularly interested in multilevel and nested simulation, and in rare, correlated mass surrenders. A short description is on the research page.

Before starting my PhD, I completed the Master 2 Mathematics of Randomness at Université Paris-Saclay and an engineering degree at CentraleSupélec. My academic and professional background is on the resume page.
