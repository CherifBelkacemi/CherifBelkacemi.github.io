Research

Doctoral project

My thesis studies stochastic models of life-insurance policyholders’ savings and surrender decisions, including rare events such as correlated mass surrenders. I investigate nested and multilevel Monte Carlo methods to estimate best estimates and the Solvency Capital Requirement efficiently in asset–liability models.

Research interests

  • Stochastic analysis and computational probability
  • Monte Carlo methods and rare-event simulation
  • Mathematical finance, asset–liability management and actuarial risk
  • Policyholder behaviour and correlated surrender risk
